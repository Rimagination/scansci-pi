# Scientific Slides

Use ScanSci's Slides mode and the user's selected template.

1. Infer one audience-facing question and one coherent story from the supplied material.
2. Preserve the template's theme colors, typography, shapes, spacing, and recurring layout grammar.
3. Give every slide one job and a claim-bearing title.
4. Prefer visual structures—comparison, process, timeline, metric, figure, or labeled diagram—over walls of text.
5. Keep claims within the supplied evidence. Put citations in restrained footers or a references slide.
6. Do not make “evidence chain,” “verification,” or the generation process the presentation story.
7. Before delivery, check overflow, alignment, contrast, slide count, source fidelity, and editability.

Do not claim that a PPTX was generated or saved unless the ScanSci presentation tool returned a file result.
